Focus-management & WCAG 2.2 AA notes
  • Mostly individual Tab stops. The top bar, +Add, browser-bar controls (Page · Connect Domain · Desktop · Mobile), the left rail, and the bottom-left panels (both in the left gutter outside the browser frame, as in the real editor) are each their own Tab stop, in order, before the Section (stops 1–25) — 2.4.3 Focus Order, 2.1.1 Keyboard.
  • The section-actions bar (#13) is ONE APG Toolbar — a single Tab stop; ←/→ rove between its buttons (roving tabindex, clamps at the ends — no wrap), Home/End jump to the first/last. Its two menu-buttons (Change Layout, Replace Background) still open their menus with ↓/Enter — a menu-button nested in a toolbar. Badged 13a (blue, the Tab stop) · 13b–13h (green, arrow-reachable) — APG Toolbar.
  • The Section is the one drill-in container (stop 26). Enter (or a mouse click) goes inside; Tab/Shift+Tab then move between its elements and its action-bar buttons (trapped + wrapping, badged purple). Merely Tab-focusing the Section does not enter it. Esc exits back to the Section. Keeps the page content out of the top-level Tab order — 2.1.2 No Keyboard Trap (Esc always exits). (Mouse click = Enter throughout.)
  • The Section action bar (top-right) is keyboard-reachable. It appears only while the Section is selected and has content on the stage (an empty section shows no bar); its buttons (Section background · Duplicate · Section layouts · Ask Aria) are Tab stops inside the drill-in, activated with Enter — so they're operable by keyboard, not just mouse — 2.1.1 Keyboard. (In the live editor this floating bar is mouse-only.)
  • Selecting an element (Enter on it) opens two surfaces at once, matching the product: the action bar above (its type-specific menu — a heading, image, and button each get different actions) and a floating small menu anchored to the element. Esc deselects and closes both. Operating those two bars by keyboard is the next phase.
  • Menu buttons (▾): open on Enter / Space / ↓ (focus first item), ↑ opens at last item. Menus wrap with ↑↓. Esc closes and returns focus to the button — 2.1.2 No Keyboard Trap, 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (aria-haspopup, aria-expanded).
  • Nothing opens on focus — only on activation — 3.2.1 On Focus.
  • Visible focus on every control — 2.4.7 Focus Visible; sticky bars must not hide the focused control — 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured.
  • Hover tooltips must also appear on keyboard focus. Every icon control that shows a tooltip on hover shows the same tooltip when it receives focus via Tab, and Esc dismisses it without moving focus — 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus, 2.1.1. (Live-editor gap: tooltips are custom hover-only components with no aria-describedby and no focus trigger.)
  • Zoom slider & section drag-handle need a keyboard alternative (arrows to change value / move) — 2.5.7 Dragging Movements. Flagged for phase 2.
  • Every icon-only control needs an accessible name — 4.1.2. (Real editor gap: rail icons often ship unnamed.)
